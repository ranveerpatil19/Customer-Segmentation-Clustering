Customer Segmentation and Clustering
Overview
This project applies unsupervised learning techniques to segment customers based on their behavior and characteristics. Using clustering algorithms such as K-Means, the project identifies distinct customer groups and provides insights that can support targeted marketing and business decision-making.

The objective is to analyze customer data and uncover meaningful patterns that help improve personalization, customer engagement, and overall business strategy.

Project Objective
Segment customers based on purchasing behavior and demographics
Identify patterns and trends within customer groups
Support data-driven marketing and customer targeting strategies
